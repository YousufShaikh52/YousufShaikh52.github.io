Privacy Policy – Thump: Heartbeat Horror

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how Thump: Heartbeat Horror (package com.indusapps.thump), published on Google Play by Indus apps ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not use the microphone or your files.

Your game progress and settings (for example nights finished, stars, notes found, embers, achievements, the daily Nightmare and its streak, the looks you own and the sound setting) are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Camera and heart rate

If you allow it, the game uses your phone's rear camera and flash while you hide, so it can read your pulse from the fingertip you rest over the lens. This is how it works:

  • In camera mode the camera runs at low resolution while you play a night (and in a hidden test screen); the flash is on only while you hide.
  • Each camera frame is reduced on your phone to three numbers (how red and how bright the middle of the picture is) and then discarded. No photo or video is ever recorded, saved or sent.
  • Your heart rate is worked out on your phone and used only to play the game. No heart data leaves your phone. The game keeps only a few game records on your device, such as your highest and calmest heart rate in the game and your "calm pulse" number, so they can appear in your Fear Report.
  • Thump is for entertainment and is not a medical device. Its heart readings are not accurate enough for any health purpose and must not be used as such.
  • The camera permission is asked the first time you hide. If you refuse it (or your phone has no rear camera or flash), the game uses rhythm mode instead, and every part of the game stays playable. You can switch modes at any time in Settings, and withdraw the permission in your Android settings.

If you share a Fear Report, the picture or text goes through your phone's own share sheet to the app you choose; we never receive it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banner ads on menu screens, video ads that you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional full-screen ads between nights (never during a night). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address
  • App interactions and diagnostics related to ads (for example ad views and clicks, crashes)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)

The camera and heart readings are never shared with advertisers or anyone else. Ad data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy (see how Google uses information from apps that use its services). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

In-app purchases

You can buy optional items (Remove ads, the Saint Agnes Ward house, a starter pack and ember packs). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The game receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, which it keeps on your device so your items stay unlocked. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Your choices

  • Camera: you can refuse or withdraw the camera permission at any time; the game then uses rhythm mode.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested we show Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.
  • Remove ads: buying Remove ads stops banners and ads between nights. Reward videos stay available, and only play when you choose them.

Children

The game is a horror game intended for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

We don't hold personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. For data processed by Google, use your Google account controls or contact Google.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
