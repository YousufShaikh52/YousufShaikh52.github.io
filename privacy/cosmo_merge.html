Privacy Policy – Cosmo Merge: Planet Drop

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Cosmo Merge: Planet Drop (package com.indusapps.cosmomerge), published on Google Play by Indus apps ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not use the camera, microphone or your files. We do not run our own servers.

Your progress (your scores, galaxy levels and stars, coins, boosters, skins and settings) is stored only on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen while you play, video ads that you choose to watch (for example for extra coins, boosters or a second chance), and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds (never in your first games). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may also fill an ad with an offer from its mediation partner Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these ad SDKs may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address
  • App interactions and diagnostics related to ads (for example ad views and clicks, crashes)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy (see how Google uses information from apps that use its services) and by Unity under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

In-app purchases

You can buy optional items: Remove ads, a Starter pack and coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The game receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, which it keeps on your device so your items stay unlocked. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested we show Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads. Your choice is shared with Unity Ads through the same consent framework. You can change it at any time in the game's settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.
  • Remove ads: buying Remove ads stops the banner and full-screen ads. Reward videos stay available and only play when you choose them.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

We don't hold personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. For data processed by Google or Unity, use your Google account controls or contact them directly.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
