Privacy Policy – Tough Nuts: Don't Crack!

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Tough Nuts: Don't Crack! (package com.indusapps.toughnuts), published on Google Play by Indus apps ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your real name, email address, contacts, precise location or any other information that identifies you. You never create an account or type a password, and the game does not use the camera, microphone or your files. We do not run our own servers.

Your progress (player name, chosen flag, trophies, coins, unlocked nuts, hats and settings) is stored only on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

Online play

When you press PLAY, the game looks for other players for a few seconds. If it finds some, you share a match with them and computer-controlled nuts (bots) fill the remaining seats; if it finds nobody, or you have no connection, every rival is computer-controlled and nothing is exchanged. Online play runs on Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay) from Unity Technologies. To make it work:

  • Anonymous online ID: when the game starts with an internet connection, Unity gives this installation a random ID. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account. A sign-in token for it is kept on your device. You can see the ID at the bottom of the game's Settings.
  • IP address: like any online service, Unity's servers see your IP address while you are connected. Unity uses it to carry the match traffic and to choose the nearest relay region. Players do not see each other's IP address: all traffic passes through Unity's relay.
  • What other players in your match receive: the player name and flag you chose in your profile, your nut and hat, your trophy count and number of matches played (used to set the difficulty), and the moment your nut lets go in each round. This is passed on live and is not stored by us. Your player name is visible to the other players, so do not use your real name.

There is no chat, no voice, no friends list and no way to contact another player. This data is processed by Unity under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and is sent over encrypted connections. If you do not want any of it, turn off Online play in the game's Settings: the game then never contacts Unity Gaming Services and every match is played against bots.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the menus, video ads that you choose to watch (for example for double coins or free coins), and an occasional full-screen ad between matches. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may also fill an ad with an offer from its mediation partner Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these ad SDKs may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address
  • App interactions and diagnostics related to ads (for example ad views and clicks, crashes)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy (see how Google uses information from apps that use its services) and by Unity under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

In-app purchases

You can buy optional items: No ads, a Starter pack, Double coins and coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The game receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, which it keeps on your device so your items stay unlocked. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested we show Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads. Your choice is shared with Unity Ads through the same consent framework. You can change it at any time in the game's settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.
  • No ads: buying No ads or the Starter pack stops the banner and full-screen ads. Reward videos stay available and only play when you choose them.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

We don't run servers of our own. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, including the sign-in token of your anonymous online ID. To have the online ID itself erased from Unity Gaming Services, email us the ID shown at the bottom of the game's Settings and we will delete it. For data processed by Google or Unity for advertising, use your Google account controls or contact them directly.
