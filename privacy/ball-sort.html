Privacy Policy – Ball Sort Puzzle

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how Ball Sort Puzzle (package com.indusapps.ballsort), published on Google Play by Indus apps ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, contacts, precise location or any other personal information. The game has no accounts and no sign-in.

Your game progress and settings (for example unlocked levels, stars, your chosen ball style, and sound and vibration preferences) are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Play statistics

To learn which levels are too hard and where players stop, the app sends anonymous play events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example that a level was started or solved, its number, moves and stars, and which optional video was watched. Firebase also records basic device information (model, Android version, language, approximate country from the IP address) and an app-instance identifier that is reset when you reinstall. The app does not let Firebase read the advertising ID, and this data is not used for advertising. It is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.

Daily reminder and sharing

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone only; nothing is sent to us. When you use Share, your phone's share sheet sends the text you chose to the app you pick; we don't receive it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may fill ad space with ads from these partner networks: AppLovin, Unity Ads and Liftoff (Vungle). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these ad SDKs may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address
  • App interactions and diagnostics related to ads (for example ad views and clicks, crashes)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy (see how Google uses information from apps that use its services) and by each partner under its own policy: AppLovin, Unity, Liftoff. Your consent choices are passed on to these partners. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch we show Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.
  • Optional ads: video ads that give rewards (hints, extra tube, more undos, skip level, a doubled chest or gift, extra Sort Rush time) are only shown when you choose to watch them.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

We don't hold personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. For data processed by Google or its ad partners, use your Google account and device ad settings, or contact them through the policies linked above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
