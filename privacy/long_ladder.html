Privacy Policy – Long Ladder: Climbing Game

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Long Ladder: Climbing Game (package com.indusapps.longladder), published on Google Play by Indus apps ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not use the camera, microphone or your files.

Your progress and settings (for example your best height, your best run's ghost, unlocked ladders, the mode you play, and sound and vibration settings) are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game passes a short text with your height to the app you pick. From then on it is handled by that app and the people you send it to, under their own privacy policies.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the home and pause menu only (never while you climb), video ads that you choose to watch for a rescue rope, and an occasional full-screen ad after you restart a climb. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address
  • App interactions and diagnostics related to ads (for example ad views and clicks, crashes)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy (see how Google uses information from apps that use its services). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

In-app purchases

You can buy optional items: Remove ads and the Ladder pack (cosmetic ladders). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The game receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, which it keeps on your device so your items stay unlocked. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested we show Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.
  • Remove ads: buying Remove ads stops the banner and full-screen ads. Reward videos stay available and only play when you choose them.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

We don't hold personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. For data processed by Google, use your Google account controls or contact Google.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
